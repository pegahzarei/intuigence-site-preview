Offerings

Engineering work, executed to completion.

Intuigents run the workflows that consume an engineering organization’s year, start to finish. Checkpoints sit exactly where your procedures require judgment: an engineer reviews, challenges, and signs. The work arrives finished, with its evidence attached.

The workflows

Five workflows. One graph underneath.

Each runs on the compiled knowledge graph of your plant: the drawings, the historian, and the documents, connected. Every claim in every deliverable cites the sheet, the tag, or the document revision it came from.

01

Process isolation

Every isolation plan is a multi-sheet trace: every line, energy source, drain, and vent feeding the work, and a missed one is a safety event. The Intuigent traces them all across the drawing set and drafts the plan for an engineer to approve. Explore

02

Root cause analysis

Investigations stall on evidence collection, and the trail cools while the backlog grows. The Intuigent builds the cited evidence file in an afternoon: topology, trends, recent changes, and prior events. Explore

03

Hazard & operability

A HAZOP consumes an expert panel for weeks, and most of those weeks go to preparation, not judgment. The Intuigent drafts the nodes, deviations, and consequences from the as-built graph, so the panel spends its time on the exceptions. Explore

04

Management of change

Every fix waits on a change review, and every review waits on an impact analysis someone has to assemble. The Intuigent classifies the change, sweeps the affected equipment and documents, and routes the package with its evidence attached. Explore

05

Turnaround scope

Scope grows because cutting a job takes evidence nobody has time to assemble. The Intuigent builds the history behind every work-list item, so each job earns its place or leaves the list. Explore

By industry

The same debt, four industries.

The backlogs differ in name, not in kind: drawings the archive cannot vouch for, reviews the bench cannot staff, and questions that wait on the one engineer who knows.

Industry, 01

Refining: the deepest backlogs in the industry, on a turnaround clock.

 Industry, 02

Chemicals: revalidation without the redraw. Hazards checked against as-built.

 Industry, 03

Energy & midstream: a wide estate, a thin bench, and an auditor waiting.

 Industry, 04

Pharmaceuticals: change control with a documented, citable trail.

How it runs

Compiled. Answered. Executed.

Every workflow above runs on the same three products, on the same graph. Buy one, and the others arrive already knowing your estate.

Compile

Cortex

Connects read-only to your historians, file stores, and drawing sets, and compiles the entire plant into one knowledge graph in under 48 hours.

Answer

Maven

Answers the engineering question in seconds, with the source attached. When it cannot verify something, it says so instead of guessing.

Execute

Marshal

Runs the workflow end to end, with human checkpoints where your procedures require sign-off, and an audit trail behind every step.
