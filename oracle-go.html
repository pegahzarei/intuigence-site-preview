Oracle GO

The control room's best answer, standing at the unit.

Oracle GO puts the same expert on a phone or tablet in the field. Crews ask from the unit and get answers against the same compiled graph the control room uses, with the same citations: the sheet, the tag, the document revision.

The answer should not live a binder run away

The question comes up at the unit. The answer lives at the desk. Everything between them is lost time and relayed detail.

  • The binder run. Walk back, find the sheet, walk out again. The job waits on the round trip.
  • The radio relay. A drawing question read aloud over radio loses the detail that made it a question. The answer that comes back is only as good as the retelling.
  • The one engineer who knows. The senior engineer cannot stand at every unit. The crew waits for a callback, or proceeds without one.

Oracle GO closes the distance. The question is asked where the work is, and the cited answer arrives there, while the crew is still standing in front of the equipment.

In the field

Where crews actually use it.

Field work is verification work: does the plant in front of you match the plant on paper. Oracle GO carries the paper.

01

The walkdown

Sheet in one hand, valve in the other, something does not match. The crew asks from the unit and gets the current revision, the tag record, and the connected lines, cited, on the spot.

02

Permit prep

Which energy sources feed this equipment, which valves isolate it, what does the drawing show upstream. The questions behind a safe permit, answered at the point of work instead of assembled back at the desk.

03

Lineup checks

Valve by valve against the drawing. The crew confirms each position against the graph as they walk the line, and every check traces back to the sheet it came from.

One graph, no field fork

The field and the desk read from the same memory

  • Same graph. One compiled plant serves the control room and the unit. There is no field copy to drift out of date.
  • Same citations. Every answer in the field carries the sheet, the tag, and the revision, exactly as it does at the desk.
  • Same honesty. When it cannot verify, it says so, in the field too. A crew at the unit needs a refusal more than anyone.
On the record. Field questions and answers are logged like every other interaction on the platform: full audit trail, per-tenant isolation, in your cloud.
