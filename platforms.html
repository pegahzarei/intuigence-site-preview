Platforms

One platform. Six products. One graph.

Every product runs on the same compiled knowledge graph of your plant. Buy one, and the others arrive already knowing your estate. This is the Industrial AI Sovereign: everything the plant has ever known, under its own command.

Cortex, Compile
01, The compile layer

Cortex

From raw plant data to a knowledge graph. Cortex connects read-only to historians, file stores, and drawing sets, and compiles the entire plant in under 24 hours: equipment, lines, instruments, documents, and the topology between them. It rebuilds as your data changes, so the graph is never an archive.

Explore Cortex

02, Ask the plant

Oracle

Engineering experts on the graph. Ask the question you would ask your best engineer, and get the answer in seconds with the source attached: the sheet, the tag, the document revision. When Oracle cannot verify something, it says so instead of guessing.

Explore Oracle

03, In the field

Oracle GO

The same expert, at the unit. Field crews ask from a phone or tablet and get cited answers against the same graph the control room uses. No binder run, no radio relay, no waiting for the one engineer who knows.

Explore Oracle GO

04, Knowledge capture

KC

Half the process-engineering workforce reaches retirement age this decade, and most of what they know was never written down. KC interviews your experts, structures what they say, and writes it into the graph as citable knowledge: the judgment behind the drawings, kept where the next engineer can find it.

Explore KC

05, The digital coworker

Twin

A coworker, not a console. Twin joins meetings and calls, answers when asked, and remembers every decision. The morning meeting stops waiting on a lookup: the answer arrives while the question is still being discussed.

Explore Twin

Marshal, Execute
06, Workflow execution

Marshal

Where answers become work. Marshal runs multi-step engineering jobs end to end: isolation plans, impact analyses, scope reviews. Human checkpoints sit exactly where your procedures require judgment and sign-off. The work arrives finished, with its evidence attached.

Explore Marshal

Why one graph

Buy one product. The rest arrive already trained.

The graph is the platform. Cortex compiles it once; Oracle, Oracle GO, KC, Twin, and Marshal all read from and write to the same structure. A fact captured in a KC interview is citable in an Oracle answer the same day. A line traced for an isolation plan is the same line Marshal uses to execute one.
