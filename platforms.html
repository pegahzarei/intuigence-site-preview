One platform. Six products. One graph.

Every product runs on the same compiled knowledge graph of your plant. Buy one, and the others arrive already knowing your estate. This is the Industrial AI Sovereign: everything the plant has ever known, under its own command.

/01

Cortex

From raw plant data to a knowledge graph. The entire plant, compiled in under 48 hours.

 /02

Maven

Engineering experts on the graph. An engineer’s answer in seconds, with the source attached.

 /03

Maven GO

The same expert, at the unit. Field crews ask; the graph answers.

 /04

KC

The judgment that was never written down, written into the graph before it retires.

 /05

Twin

A digital coworker. Joins meetings and calls, answers when asked, remembers every decision.

 /06

Marshal

Multi-step engineering jobs, run end to end with human checkpoints.

Why one graph

Buy one product. The rest arrive already trained.

The graph is the platform. Cortex compiles it once; Maven, Maven GO, KC, Twin, and Marshal all read from and write to the same structure. A fact captured in a KC interview is citable in a Maven answer the same day. A line traced for an isolation plan is the same line Marshal uses to execute one.
