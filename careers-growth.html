Careers

Growth Hacker

We're looking for a highly intelligent, resourceful growth hacker to own early lead generation and support product-led growth experiments.

Entry level , SoCal / Remote , Contract based , Posted November 30, 2025

About us

We're an AI-native startup backed by top-tier VCs (including Eric Schmidt's fund). Our team includes Berkeley AI Lab researchers, MAANG engineers, and repeat founders. We're building synthetic AI engineers for industrial users, starting with mechanical, chemical, electrical, and EPC engineers. Our customers include some of the largest energy and manufacturing companies in the world.

We've proven value in top-down deployments. Now, we're activating our bottom-up, product-led motion.

About the role

We're looking for a highly intelligent, resourceful growth hacker to own early lead generation and support product-led growth experiments. You'll work side-by-side with product and GTM teams to reach technical users in clever and efficient ways. Part time to start, moving to full time as ROI shows. Entry to mid level, with high autonomy and high upside.

Responsibilities

  • Scrape or source high-signal leads from LinkedIn, Reddit, forums, conference lists, and Slack/Discord groups
  • Launch and test outbound/email campaigns, referral workflows, and landing page variations
  • Suggest experiments for onboarding, signup, or virality that can help grow our free user base

First 30 days

  • Deliver 100+ qualified leads
  • Identify the 5 most active communities where the ideal users spend time
  • Launch 3+ outbound/email campaigns with tailored segments
  • Contribute ideas on optimizing landing page, onboarding, or referrals based on experiments and feedback

Requirements

  • You're sharp, self-directed, and move fast
  • You've grown something from zero. Could be a newsletter, a side tool, or a Discord group
  • You write clearly and persuasively, especially for technical audiences
  • Bonus: you understand AI-native products and industrial workflows

Who should not apply

  • If you need a fully built growth playbook (we're early)
  • If you prefer top-down direction instead of figuring things out
  • If you're looking for a big team and mature brand (this is a zero-to-one stage)

What's in it for you

  • Elite peers: direct access to world-class operators, engineers, and SMEs
  • Ownership: test wild ideas. Build your own stack. Run your own playbook
  • Startup velocity: fast cycles, visible results, high upside
  • Direct impact: your work will land in the hands of Fortune 100 engineers
  • Trajectory: path to a full-time role if successful

What to include

  • Your resume
  • Top 5 of our direct competitors for this PLG motion
  • One unconventional place you'd look for our target users, and how you'd reach them

Apply

Send us your resume and something you built.
