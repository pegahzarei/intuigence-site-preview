Our Software

Maven

Ask the question you would ask your best engineer.

Maven answers in seconds with the source attached: the sheet, the tag, the document revision. It reasons over the compiled graph of your plant, not a pile of documents. And when it cannot verify something, it says so instead of guessing.

The most expensive search bar in industry is an engineer's day

40% of an engineer's day is spent finding information the plant already has. The information exists. It is just spread across sheets, historians, and document systems that do not talk to each other.

  • “Trace this line across all sheets.” A multi-sheet trace through off-page connectors, done by hand, consumes a senior engineer's afternoon.
  • “What is upstream of this exchanger?” The answer is drawn, not written. A text search cannot find it.
  • “Did the controller run above its set point last month?” The historian knows. Getting it out means finding the right tag first.
  • “List every relief valve on this line and its set pressure.” Sheets plus datasheets plus revisions: a day of cross-referencing.

Each of these returns in seconds against the graph, cited to the drawing, the tag, or the document revision it came from. Verification is one click, not a binder run.

In practice

Where the seconds matter.

The same question costs different things at different moments. These are the moments Maven was built for.

01

The morning meeting

An overnight deviation raises a question about the loop behind it. The answer arrives, cited, while the item is still on the table, not as an action item due Thursday.

02

The auditor's question

An auditor asks for the basis of a relief-valve setting. Maven returns the datasheet, the drawing, and the revision history: the evidence, not a recollection of it.

03

The deviation

A reading looks wrong. Maven pulls the loop, the trend, and the sheets around it, so the engineer starts from evidence instead of assembling it.

04

The line trace

One line, many sheets, every off-page connector resolved. The trace that took a day of sheet-turning takes seconds, and every hop cites its drawing.

Grounded by architecture

Trust is a design decision

In a plant, an honest refusal outranks a confident guess. Maven is built to that standard.

  • Every answer cites its source. The sheet, the tag, the document revision. Engineers verify instead of taking the machine's word.
  • When it cannot verify, it says so. No source on the graph means no claim in the answer. That behavior is enforced by architecture, not by prompt.
  • Accuracy holds as the estate grows. The graph carries the context a chat window drops. Two hundred drawings or twenty thousand, the answer stays grounded.
Measured in the open. The benchmark methodology is public, and the harness runs on your own drawings. Not our demo set: yours.
