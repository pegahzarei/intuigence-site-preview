Senior level , Remote - Global (must have significant overlap with US time zones) , Part time , Posted July 14, 2026

About the role

We're looking for a Product Designer with strong interaction design skills to help shape how our users experience the product: from micro-interactions to complex flows. You'll play a key role in bringing our interfaces to life with motion, depth, and clarity, including expressive 3D and isometric visual elements.

Responsibilities

  • Design end-to-end product experiences with a strong focus on interaction design and usability
  • Create and prototype micro-interactions, transitions, and motion systems
  • Develop 3D isometric illustrations and assets to enrich the product's visual language
  • Collaborate closely with engineers, PMs, and other designers to ship polished features
  • Build and maintain interactive prototypes to communicate design intent clearly
  • Contribute to and evolve the design system

Requirements

  • Strong portfolio demonstrating interaction design craft (not just visual UI)
  • Solid understanding of 3D isometric design principles and asset creation
  • Experience with Blender for 3D modeling and rendering
  • Proficiency with Framer for interactive, high-fidelity prototyping
  • Comfortable working in Figma (or similar) for core UI/UX design
  • Strong sense of motion, timing, and micro-interaction detail
  • Ability to think in systems and maintain consistency across a product

Nice to have

  • Experience designing for SaaS or B2B products
  • Familiarity with front-end basics (CSS/animation libraries) for closer dev handoff
  • Background in illustration or 3D art
