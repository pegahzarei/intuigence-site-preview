Company

Your cloud. Your tenant. Your graph.

Industrial knowledge is competitive and safety-critical. The deployment model is built so that it never has to leave your command: compiled inside your estate, isolated per tenant, auditable end to end.

The model

01

Deployed in your estate

Intuigents run inside your own cloud environment, deployable from Azure Marketplace and native to Microsoft Fabric. Your security team governs the environment with the controls it already operates.

02

Read-only connections

Source systems are connected read-only: historians, file stores, and drawing sets stay where they are and as they are. Compilation happens beside your data, not after an export to someone else's.

03

Per-tenant isolation

Your graph is compiled from your data and serves only your tenant. No cross-customer sharing, no training a shared model on your plant for someone else's benefit.

04

Citations on every answer

Every claim traces to the sheet, tag, or document revision it came from, so any answer can be checked by a human before it is acted on. When something cannot be verified, the Intuigent says so.

05

Audit trail end to end

Questions, answers, checkpoints, and executed steps are logged. What was asked, what was cited, who approved: the record is complete and yours.

Human command

Checkpoints are a security control.

Work-executing AI raises a fair question: who decides? The answer is built into the workflow layer. Multi-step jobs pause at the points where your procedures require judgment, and a named person approves before the work proceeds. Intuigents do the legwork; your people keep authority over decisions that touch the plant.
