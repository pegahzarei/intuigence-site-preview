Company

We build engineers that don't retire.

IntuigenceAI gives industrial operators a workforce of synthetic engineers: Intuigents, built to think and work like the chemical, plant, electrical, and mechanical experts the industry can no longer hire fast enough.

Why we exist

Industry is losing engineering capacity faster than it can hire it back. Half the process-engineering workforce retires this decade, and the backlog behind them only grows. The market answered with chatbots and six-month integration projects. We built synthetic engineers that do the work.

What we're building

Domain-specific engineers that solve real industrial problems through deep expertise, safe automation, and human oversight. Synthetic intelligence runs the work, from planning to execution. Your people stay in command, freed for the judgment only they can make.

The category: work-executing industrial AI

Most industrial AI stops at an answer. Intuigents finish the job: they answer, then execute. That is the category we build in, and the difference between a tool you operate and an engineer who does the work. Read the definition

The team

Founded by Moe Tanabian.

Former Global Vice President of Industrial Software at Microsoft. The team has built and shipped the cloud, AI, and industrial-software platforms this category runs on, at Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, and Samsung. It is the rare pairing of frontier AI and heavy-industry engineering that this problem demands, and the reason Intuigents work in a real plant rather than a demo.
