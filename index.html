The problem

The work isn't going away. The engineers are.

The intelligence of a plant is trapped in its drawings. A P&ID is topology: the connections and sequence between thousands of symbols. Generic models read the text and stop there, about 5% of what the sheet holds. The rest takes an expert's eye, one sheet at a time, across tens of thousands of sheets. And the experts are leaving: most of what they know was never written down.

5%of a P&ID is text. Generic models stop there.

Hiring will not close the gap. The backlog of isolations, investigations, hazard reviews, and change requests grows behind engineers the market cannot supply.
