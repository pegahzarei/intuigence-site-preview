Industries

Change control, with the trail attached.

In a validation-heavy environment, the change itself is the fast part. The impact analysis, the document sweep, and the citable record behind them are what consume the calendar. An Intuigent delivers all three in hours, with every claim traced to source.

The weight of a single change

In GMP-regulated operations, no change is small. A pump swap, a utility reroute, a setpoint revision: each one triggers the same questions, and each question demands a documented answer.

  • What does this touch? The affected equipment, lines, instruments, and utilities, traced through the actual topology, not the tribal memory of it.
  • Which documents must change? Procedures, qualification records, drawings, and training material that reference the affected systems, found by sweep rather than by recollection.
  • How do we show our work? Every conclusion in the assessment needs a source someone can open. A trail that lives in one engineer’s inbox is not a trail.

The work is not hard engineering. It is exhaustive engineering, and exhaustive is exactly what a thin bench cannot staff.

The workflow

The impact analysis, built from the graph.

Cortex compiles your drawings, documents, and process data into one knowledge graph. The change assessment runs against that graph, so the sweep is complete and every finding arrives cited.

01

Scope the change

Traces the affected systems through the as-built topology: upstream, downstream, shared utilities, and the instrument loops in between.

02

Sweep the documents

Finds every procedure, drawing, and record that references the scoped systems. The list arrives with document numbers and revisions, not guesses.

03

Draft the assessment

Assembles the impact analysis with a citation behind every claim. Where something cannot be verified from the record, it is flagged as open, not papered over.

04

Route with checkpoints

The package moves through review with human sign-off at every gate your procedures define, and every step logged end to end.

What an Intuigent does not do. It does not certify compliance, and it does not replace your quality unit. It assembles the evidence: the trace, the sweep, and the citations behind the decision your people sign.
