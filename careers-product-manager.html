Product Manager - Applied LLM/AI for Industrial Operations

We seek a mission-driven Product Manager to lead the development of AI products that reshape industrial productivity. You'll translate strategic vision into actionable product roadmaps, own end-to-end execution, and drive adoption through both product-led growth (PLG) and enterprise channels. This role demands ownership, precision, and a passion for solving complex industrial challenges.

Mid level , Bay Area or South California preferred , Full time , Posted August 12, 2025

About us

We are an innovative startup at the forefront of applied artificial intelligence. Our mission is to build “synthetic engineers”: highly specialized AI agents designed to tackle critical challenges within the manufacturing sector. By creating a new generation of digital engineering expertise, we are helping companies enhance productivity, solve complex problems, and drive future innovation. We are looking for passionate individuals to join us in building the future of manufacturing.

About the role

We seek a mission-driven Product Manager to lead the development of AI products that reshape industrial productivity. You'll translate strategic vision into actionable product roadmaps, own end-to-end execution, and drive adoption through both product-led growth (PLG) and enterprise channels. This role demands ownership, precision, and a passion for solving complex industrial challenges. Preferred location: Bay Area or Southern California. Exceptional remote candidates will be considered.

Responsibilities

  • Execute product strategy: turn product vision and strategy into detailed PRDs, roadmaps, and release plans for AI-driven industrial solutions
  • Drive growth: optimize core metrics (DAU, TTS, TTFV) for the PLG motion while supporting enterprise customer success
  • Drive cross-functional execution: partner closely with engineering, design, and QA teams to ship solutions from concept to launch
  • Own the product lifecycle: manage sprint planning (via Linear), prioritization, and post-launch iteration for continuous value delivery
  • Champion industrial AI: deeply understand oil/gas operations to build solutions that solve real-world engineering challenges

Requirements

  • Entrepreneurial: you take full ownership, think strategically, and thrive in a fast-paced startup environment
  • Customer-obsessed: you're driven to understand customer needs and deliver solutions that solve their toughest challenges
  • Intellectually curious: you're fascinated by AI, LLMs, and industrial operations, with a commitment to mastering complex domains
  • Experienced: 3-5 years of product management experience with a proven ability to lead development and write actionable PRDs
  • Technically adept: deep understanding of AI systems and generative AI, with demonstrated commitment to tracking the latest advancements

Nice to have

  • PM experience at leading AI/ML or industrial/energy companies
  • Knowledge of oil/gas or manufacturing operations
  • Ability to read code and collaborate effectively with technical teams

Ready to build the future?

The world's largest industrial players are already working with us, and we're only getting started. This is a rare opportunity to shape and scale 1B synthetic engineers and fundamentally rewire how industrial operations run.

We're looking for builders who are entrepreneurial, customer-obsessed, and driven by the frontier of applied technology in industrial systems. If that sounds like you, we'd love to hear from you.
