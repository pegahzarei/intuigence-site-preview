The doctrine

Everything the plant has ever known, under its own command.

A plant's knowledge is its most durable asset and its least usable one. The sovereignty doctrine says that changes on your terms: compiled inside your estate, answering to your people, owned by no one else.

What sovereignty means here

Industrial AI keeps failing on the same three promises: the data leaves, the knowledge rents, the vendor holds the keys. The Industrial AI Sovereign is the opposite commitment, stated plainly.

  • Your cloud, your tenant. The graph is compiled inside your own estate, deployable from Azure Marketplace, Microsoft Fabric-native. It serves your plant and no one else's. Nothing trains on your data for someone else's benefit.
  • Read-only in, evidence out. Intuigents connect read-only to the historians, file stores, and drawing sets you already run. They do not move your data; they compile it where it lives, and every answer carries citations back to it.
  • The knowledge compounds for you. Every captured interview, every traced line, every closed investigation makes your graph more valuable. That value accrues to the plant, not to a vendor's foundation model.

Why now

The expertise is leaving faster than it can be replaced.

Half the process-engineering workforce reaches retirement age this decade. Most of what they know was never written down. The market's answer has been chatbots that summarize documents and platforms that take half a year to stand up. Neither holds the plant's knowledge, and neither does the work.

The doctrine in practice

01

Compile, don't index

A searchable archive is not a model of the plant. Cortex compiles drawings, historians, and documents into one knowledge graph: the topology, not just the text. Cortex

02

Cite, don't assert

Every answer traces to the sheet, the tag, the revision. When something cannot be verified, the Intuigent says so instead of guessing. Oracle

03

Capture before it retires

The judgment that lives in your senior engineers' heads is written into the graph as citable knowledge, while they are still in the building. KC

04

Execute, don't just answer

The value is in the work that follows the answer. Multi-step engineering jobs run end to end, with human checkpoints where your procedures require judgment. Marshal

05

Keep command human

Intuigents do the legwork. Your people weigh the evidence, make the calls, and sign the work. Sovereignty includes sovereignty over decisions.

The plants that win the next decade will not be the ones that bought the most AI. They will be the ones that still command their own knowledge.
