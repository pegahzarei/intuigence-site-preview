IntuigenceAI at CERAWeek 2026.

Houston, March 2026. A week of talks and sessions on sovereign industrial AI, alongside the operators and executives who run the world’s energy system.

CERAWeek gathers the energy industry’s decision makers in one place, once a year. We came with a simple argument: the engineering workforce is retiring faster than it can be replaced, and the answer is not another chatbot. It is a synthetic workforce that does the work, inside the operator’s own estate.

Across the week’s talks and sessions, the same three conversations kept returning: the retirement cliff and the backlog it leaves behind, what it takes to compile a plant’s drawings and data into something a machine can reason over, and why sovereignty over industrial knowledge is becoming a boardroom question rather than an IT one.

To every operator, partner, and skeptic who sat down with us in Houston: thank you. The skeptics made the week.
