Our Software

Marshal

Answers are good. Finished work is better.

Marshal runs multi-step engineering jobs end to end: isolation plans, impact analyses, scope reviews. Human checkpoints sit exactly where your procedures require judgment and sign-off. The work arrives finished, with its evidence attached.

Most industrial AI stops at an answer

The backlog is not made of questions. It is made of work: isolations to plan, investigations to close, hazard reviews to run, change requests to process. The economic value lives in the work that follows the answer.

  • Briefed the way an engineer is briefed. Marshal plans each job from three sources, in order: the operator's request, the plant's own procedures, and standard practice. Where the plant has a procedure, Marshal follows it. Where it does not, it presents its plan first.
  • Grounded at every step. Every intermediate result cites its source on the graph: the sheet, the tag, the document revision. The audit trail writes itself as the work runs.
  • Finished means finished. The deliverable is the work product an engineer would deliver: the plan, the study, the analysis, ready for sign-off.

Division of labor

MarshalRuns the job

  • Traces the topology, pulls the history, sweeps the documents
  • Drafts the plan, the study, or the analysis, cited throughout
  • Assembles the evidence package as the work runs
  • Stops at every checkpoint and presents what it has

Your teamOwns the judgment

  • Chooses which steps require an engineer's sign-off
  • Reviews the evidence at each checkpoint and approves or redirects
  • Signs the finished work, with the record attached
Checkpoints where judgment lives. The checkpoint is not a disclaimer. Marshal stops at the steps your procedures reserve for an engineer, presents the evidence, and waits. Nothing proceeds past a checkpoint without a human decision, and every decision is logged.
