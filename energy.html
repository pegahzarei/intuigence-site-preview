Industries

Wide estate, thin bench.

Digitize at scale. Trace any line across the network. Answer the auditor the same day. That is the mandate for an estate of gathering systems, terminals, and stations, served by an engineering organization a fraction of the size a single refinery keeps on site.

The pressure, Distance

The estate problem.

A refinery’s trace crosses sheets. A midstream trace crosses facilities: the line leaves one site’s drawing set and enters another’s, and no engineer holds both in their head. With a handful of engineers covering dozens of assets, every integrity question, every audit request, and every change review travels to the same short list of people. The drawing debt is measured in decades, and the deadline is measured in days.

What is P&ID digitization?

Workflows for energy & midstream

One graph for the whole network.

Cortex compiles each site’s drawings, documents, and historian into one knowledge graph, in under 48 hours per connection. The network becomes a single queryable structure: the trace that crossed three facilities becomes one question.

01

Digitize at scale

Full drawing sets across the estate, compiled without moving or rewriting anything. Read-only connections; the source systems stay the source systems.

02

Trace across the network

Any line, followed from site to site with the sheets cited at every hop. The question that waited on the one engineer who knew now takes seconds.

03

Answer the auditor

Integrity and compliance questions answered the same day, with the drawing, the tag, and the document revision attached to every claim.

04

Run the reviews

Change requests, isolation plans, and investigations executed on the graph, with checkpoints where your procedures require an engineer’s signature.
